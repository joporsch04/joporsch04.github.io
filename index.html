I am a Master’s student in Physics at ETH Zürich focusing on theoretical physics. My studies center on formal aspects of quantum field theory, specifically effective field theories (EFTs), with strong interests in the bootstrap program and integrability.

Prior to ETH, I completed my B.Sc. in Physics at LMU Munich with a thesis on strong-field ionization dynamics, and worked as a research assistant at the Max Planck Institute of Quantum Optics.
